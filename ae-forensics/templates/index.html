How to Download a .eml File (Gmail Guide)

          

          
Follow these quick steps on your laptop or computer to export any email for security analysis.

        

        
      


      
      

        
        
        

          
1

          

            
Open Gmail on your Laptop or Computer

            

              Open your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox) and log into your Gmail account.
            

            

              ⚠️ Note: The Gmail mobile app does not support downloading raw email (.eml) files directly. Please use a laptop or desktop browser.
            

          

        


        
        

          
2

          

            
Open the Email you want to inspect

            

              Click on the specific email message in your inbox to open and view it.
            

          

        


        
        

          
3

          

            
Click the Three Dots (More Options ⋮)

            

              Look at the top-right corner of the email message (next to the Reply arrow), and click the three vertical dots (⋮) icon.
            

          

        


        
        

          
4

          

            
Click "Show original"

            

              From the dropdown menu, click "Show original". A new browser tab will immediately open showing the raw email headers and structure.
            

          

        


        
        

          
5

          

            
Click "Download Original"

            

              On the new tab, click the blue "Download Original" button to save the file to your computer.
            

            

              💡 Tip: Make sure to save the file with a .eml extension (e.g., invoice.eml). If it saves as .txt, it will still work, but .eml is recommended.
            

          

        


        
        

          
6

          

            
Upload & Analyze on AE-Forensics

            

              Drag and drop your downloaded .eml file into our scanner above to get an instant threat score, security checks, and full transmission server route map!
            

          

        


      


      
      

        Supports all standard .eml, .msg, and .txt files
